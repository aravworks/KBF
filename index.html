Our Story

Three decades of craftsmanship, brick by brick

For over 30 years, Khalsa Brick Fields has manufactured premium red bricks from Kalyanpur, Kanpur — using high-quality yellow soil and traditional coal-fired kilns. Every single brick passes through 200–300 skilled hands before it's ready, a level of care that's earned us the trust of government contractors and builders across the city.

  • ✓ Low water absorption for long-lasting strength
  • ✓ Consistent size & colour, batch after batch

From Soil to Brick

Our manufacturing process

01

High-Quality Soil

We source fertile yellow soil from local farmland — the foundation of a strong, consistent brick.

02

Hand-Moulding

Every brick is shaped by hand. A single brick passes through 200–300 skilled workers before it's ready to fire.

03

Natural Drying

Green bricks are air-dried naturally before they enter the kiln.

04

Coal-Fired Kiln

Fired in traditional coal-fed kilns for the strength and deep red colour our bricks are known for.
