What We Manufacture

A brick for every build

Red Clay Bricks

Wire-cut, kiln-fired bricks for structural walls and load-bearing masonry.

  • Size230 × 110 × 75 mm
  • Compressive Strength7–10 N/mm²
  • Water Absorption< 15%

Fly Ash Bricks

Eco-friendly, uniform bricks with low breakage in transit.

  • Size230 × 110 × 75 mm
  • Compressive Strength10–12 N/mm²
